How UniMeet works

Go from an empty Friday evening to confirmed plans in three straightforward steps.

01

Pick your interests & city

Create an account in under a minute, select your favorite music, cinema, gaming, or outdoor interests, and optionally add your university.

02

Swipe events, people, or groups

Explore curated city outings, join open groups, or post a Going Solo status when you want company for a specific night.

03

Coordinate in real-time chat

Once a mutual match, group join, or solo meetup is confirmed, agree on a public meeting spot in live WebSocket messaging.

Built-in safety & moderation controls

Every profile and conversation includes one-tap reporting and instant blocking. Blocked users disappear from discovery, groups, Going Solo feeds, and chat immediately.

Accounts are email-registered without fabricated identity verification badges, and first meetups are encouraged in public, well-lit campus or city venues.
